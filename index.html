Zelda NES Recreated with Custom Level

Play Now

An authentic recreation of the original 1986 NES Legend of Zelda's first dungeon + an original custom level where you can capture the Keese enemy for a series of puzzle platforming challenges.

The game was developed using Unity and C#. For the recreation of the original game I worked on implementing Link's movement, the health and damage systems, and the Stalfos, Keese, Goriya, and Bladetrap enemies, amongst other tasks. For the custom level I focused on building out tools like the pressure plates and Keese spawner, and collaborated on the level design and progression

Controls: Movement - WASD/Arrow keys, Primary Attack - X, Secondary Attack - Z, Swap Weapons - Space, Start Custom Level - 4

Tilt: Puzzle Platformer

Play Now

Tilt is a puzzle platformer revolving around a tilting mechanic where you move the entire level. Inspired by ball maze puzzles, take on a series of increasingly challenging levels as you make your way through the game.

The game was developed using Unity and C#. I implemented every part of the game from the ground up.

Controls: Movement - WASD/Arrow keys, God Mode - G

Ghosting Together

Play Now

Ghosting Together: A cooperative puzzle-platformer where two ghosts search for their lost friend trapped in the heart of a dark haunted mansion full of terrors

The game was developed using Unity and C#, in a team of 4. We developed most of our assets from scratch and had some assistance from freelancers for art and audio.
